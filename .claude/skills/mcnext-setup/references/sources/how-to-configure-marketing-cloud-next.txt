## Identity Resolution Rulesets #
Trello Card: https://trello.com/c/lWwU15Kt/2-user-story-12-identity-resolution-rulesets
Loom: https://www.loom.com/share/ef6ff9c8ad684e1bbfe6b693a04b212b?sid=e900c5ea-ce84-4aec-9610-93e08288bfc0
As Marketing Cloud Growth relies on Data Cloud Technology. The way Marketing Cloud Growth Identify email subscribers is by using ‘Rule Sets’ – A group of rules that allows us to identify an individual based on a range of criteria i.e Email Address, Phone Number, First Name, Last Name.
Marketing Cloud Growth allows you to generate a Rule Set for Individual automatically by clicking on ‘Generate Ruleset’. The default Identity Resolution Ruleset is based on Email Address matching only – which is more than enough to start, in the future you can build more complex rulesets.

## Einstein #
Einstein features are leveraging Artificial intelligence to give you better insights on your Marketing Efforts.
Optimize Email Send Times
Einstein Send Time Optimization is a predictive AI feature that uses historical data to help you send emails on the days and times when your customers are most likely to open them. To use this feature in Marketing Cloud Next Growth Edition, you must opt in to the Global Data Model for Einstein. In Advanced Edition, you can enable global models or disable them to opt out.
Filter Non-Human Clicks from Your Report
Einstein Metrics Guard uses predictive AI to filter bot activity from your org’s engagement metrics. It removes most clicks and opens generated by security scanner bots without filtering out legitimate visitor activity.
Increase Customer Engagement with Einstein Engagement Scoring
Einstein Engagement Scoring is a predictive AI feature that uses historical data to assign individual contact-level scores to determine the most efficient way to message recipients to increase customer engagement. This feature is available only in Marketing Cloud Next Advanced Edition.
Analyze Behavioral Data with Einstein Engagement Frequency
Einstein Engagement Frequency is a predictive AI feature that aggregates behavior data to determine how frequently to contact customers and improve the likelihood of customer engagement. This feature is available only in Marketing Cloud Next Advanced edition.
